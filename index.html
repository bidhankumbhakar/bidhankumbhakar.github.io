Electrocatalysis

COF-based Electrocatalytic Materials

🎯 Key Focus

Oxygen Reduction Reaction (ORR) using vinylene-linked COF electrocatalysts on carbon support, achieving 4e⁻ pathway for clean electricity generation.

📊 Key Results

Half-wave potential close to Pt/C standard, excellent durability in alkaline media, high selectivity toward 4e⁻ ORR pathway.

🧪 Materials Used

Metal-embedded COFs (Fe, Co, Ni nodes), vinylene-linked frameworks, Pd-anchored COF for HER, Ag-NP functionalized COF.

📄 Key Papers

JACS 2026 (Woven COF-ORR), Nanoscale 2026 (Pd-COF), ACS Appl. Nano Mater. 2025 (AgNP-COF).

"Designing next-generation electrocatalysts using the structural tunability of COFs to achieve platinum-group-metal-free catalysis for sustainable energy conversion."

☀

Photocatalysis

Light-Driven Catalytic Reactions

🎯 Key Focus

H₂O₂ production via 2e⁻ ORR pathway under visible light, aerobic organic transformations, and photocatalytic hydrogen evolution.

📊 Key Results

H₂O₂ production rate >1200 μmol g⁻¹ h⁻¹, aerobic amine oxidation >98% yield, excellent recyclability and stability.

🧪 Materials Used

Donor-acceptor COFs, dimolybdenum paddlewheel-embedded frameworks, Chem. Commun. D-A COFs, Adv. Materials COF series.

📄 Key Papers

Small 2025 (Mo-paddlewheel COF), Chem. Commun. 2025 (D-A COF), Adv. Materials 2025, ACS Mat. Lett. 2024.

"Harnessing sunlight through precisely engineered COF architectures to drive selective chemical transformations — from solar fuel production to green organic synthesis."

🔋

Energy Storage

Supercapacitors & Battery Systems

🎯 Key Focus

COF-based supercapacitor electrodes, Li-S battery cathode materials, and high-energy-density porous carbon from biomass (bamboo).

📊 Key Results

Specific capacitance >300 F g⁻¹, cycle stability >10,000 cycles, amorphous vs crystalline COF comparison for superior energy storage.

🧪 Materials Used

Redox-active COFs, nitrogen-rich frameworks, superhydrophobic biochar from bamboo, Angew. Chem. Li-S COF hosts.

📄 Key Papers

ACS Appl. Energy Mater. 2023, Angew. Chem. 2023 (Li-S), Cryst. Growth Des. 2023, J. Mat. Chem. A 2026 (biochar).

"Engineering the pore environment of COFs for optimal charge storage — bridging the gap between high power density and high energy density for next-gen batteries and supercapacitors."

🔬

COF Design & Synthesis

Covalent Organic Framework Engineering

🎯 Key Focus

Design, synthesis, and characterization of crystalline porous COFs with imine, vinylene, and β-ketoenamine linkages for multifunctional applications.

📊 Key Results

BET surface areas >1500 m² g⁻¹, controlled pore sizes 1–4 nm, interlocking woven topology with 3.6 nm inter-chain spacing.

🧪 Techniques

PXRD, BET N₂ sorption, TEM/SEM, TGA, solid-state NMR, XPS, UV-Vis DRS, EPR spectroscopy.

📄 Key Papers

JACS 2026 (woven COF), Cryst. Growth Des. 2023 (crystalline vs amorphous), all 12 publications involve COF design.

"COFs are the future of functional porous materials — their crystallinity, tunability, and stability make them ideal platforms for heterogeneous catalysis and energy applications."
